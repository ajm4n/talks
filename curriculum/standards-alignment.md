# Standards Alignment

This course is designed to map to widely used frameworks so it can satisfy CTE pathway, computer-science, and cybersecurity program requirements. Adapt the specific codes to your state's standards.

## Frameworks referenced

- **NICE Framework** — NIST SP 800-181r1, the Workforce Framework for Cybersecurity (work roles, tasks, knowledge, skills).
- **CSTA K-12 CS Standards** — Computer Science Teachers Association, levels 3A/3B (grades 9–12).
- **NSA/CISA CAE-CD Knowledge Units** — Centers of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense, foundational/technical KUs.
- **CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) domains** — for students who may pursue early certification.
- **CyberSeek / NICE Cybersecurity Workforce** — for career-pathway awareness.

## NICE Framework work roles emphasized
- **Vulnerability Assessment Analyst** (PR-VAM-001)
- **Penetration Tester** (offensive testing tasks)
- **Cyber Defense Analyst** (PR-CDA-001) — the defensive flip side
- **Secure Software Developer** (awareness, via web-vuln units)

## Security+ readiness note
This course is **not** a Security+ bootcamp, but completing it gives students a strong foundation in Security+ domains 1, 2, and parts of 3 and 4 — especially the threat/vulnerability and general-security-concepts content. Pair with a dedicated Security+ study guide if certification is a program goal.

## 21st-century / employability skills
Throughout, students practice: structured problem-solving, documentation and technical writing, ethical reasoning, collaboration (pair labs), and professional communication (the capstone report + presentation).
