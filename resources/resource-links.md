# Resource Links

Curated, mostly-free resources to support the course. Verify links and your school's content-filter allowlist before relying on any platform.

## Hands-on lab platforms
- **TryHackMe** — guided, browser-based cybersecurity rooms; free tier + education discounts. *Primary platform for most units.*
- **Hack The Box / HTB Academy** — guided modules (Academy) and practice machines (HTB); student/academic options.
- **picoCTF** (Carnegie Mellon) — free beginner CTF + year-round practice gym. *Great for capstone.*
- **OverTheWire** — free SSH-based wargames; *Bandit* is the classic Linux/command-line starter.
- **VulnHub** — free downloadable vulnerable VMs for local labs.
- **DVWA** — Damn Vulnerable Web Application, for the web units.
- **Metasploitable 2** — intentionally vulnerable Linux VM for scanning/exploitation.
- **PortSwigger Web Security Academy** — free, excellent web-vulnerability labs (pairs with Units 10–12).

## Tools (all free / open source)
- **Kali Linux** — the attack-workstation distro (pre-built VirtualBox image available).
- **VirtualBox** — free virtualization for local labs.
- **Wireshark** — packet capture/analysis.
- **nmap** — network scanning.
- **Burp Suite (Community)** — web proxy/testing.
- **Hydra, John the Ripper, Hashcat** — password attacks.
- **Metasploit Framework** — exploitation framework.
- **Exploit-DB / searchsploit** — public exploit archive.

## Reference & reading
- **OWASP Top 10** — the standard web-risk list.
- **MITRE ATT&CK** — knowledge base of attacker tactics & techniques (great for mapping the attack lifecycle).
- **CVE / NVD** — public vulnerability databases.
- **NIST SP 800-115** — Technical Guide to Information Security Testing and Assessment (professional methodology).
- **OffSec PEN-200 / OSCP** — the professional course this curriculum is adapted from.

## Careers & certifications
- **CyberSeek** — interactive map of cybersecurity careers, salaries, and pathways.
- **NICE Framework** — the cybersecurity workforce framework.
- **CompTIA Security+** — entry-level certification (good next step after this course).
- **OffSec OSCP** — advanced, hands-on pentesting certification.
- **National Cyber League (NCL)** & **CyberPatriot** — student competitions to extend learning.

## For instructors
- **NSA/CISA CAE-CD** — Centers of Academic Excellence resources and knowledge units.
- **CISA cybersecurity education resources** — free K-12 materials.
- **CSTA** — computer science teaching standards and community.
- **Teach Cyber / GenCyber** — curricula, professional development, and summer programs for cyber educators.

## Ethics & law
- **Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (18 U.S.C. § 1030)** — read the statute summary with students (Unit 1).
- **EFF resources on the CFAA & security research** — accessible explainers.
- **disclose.io / bug-bounty program directories** — examples of legal vulnerability disclosure.

> ⚠️ Always pre-check that any external site is appropriate and accessible on your school network, and that account creation complies with student-privacy rules (COPPA/FERPA) and platform age requirements.
